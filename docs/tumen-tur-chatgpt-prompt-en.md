Task: produce **one unified company logo** for **Tumen Tur** (tumentur.ru) that works on the website header, a letterhead, Telegram, and an airport meeting sign. Then critique your own work with yes/no tables and redraw what fails. Do not defend the first pass. Do not deliver a 9-style moodboard.

## 2. What is already on the live site (and why it fails)

The current emblem (attachment 1) stacks: alpine peaks + water sparkle + compass rose + bird + **Lake Baikal outline glued on the side**. At ~40 px in a header this is mush. It reads “mountain club / 2010s outdoor kit”, not “local Irkutsk guides”.

## 3. What the unified logo is (three layers, not three pictures)

Three geographies are **not** drawn as three pictures in one emblem. That is what the live site already did. It dies small.

| Layer | Content | Form | Where it lives |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Mark** | Lake Baikal contour **from the site** | Attachment 2 / 3: SW–NE elongated checkmark, even thickness, characteristic kink, NE peninsula. Not a new oval. | Favicon, stamp, left of the name |
| **Name** | «Тумэн Тур» | Cyrillic. The letter **э** in Тумэн is U+044D (Cyrillic e), intact, not Latin E, not Greek. Same point size for both words. Sturdy grotesque. | Always next to the mark on header and letterhead |
| **Destination line** | «Байкал · Алтай · Монголия» | Words, not icons. Smaller than the name. Outside the emblem. | Letterhead and wide desktop only. **Hide on site header / mobile.** |

**Altai in the company logo = the word «Алтай».** Not hills inside the mark. A ridge pictogram (2–3 receding planes, not the letter M, not Alps, not a house roof) is **only** for a destination page.

**Mongolia in the company logo = the word «Монголия».** No ger, no Soyombo, no horse, no second lake (Khövsgöl), no steppe ornament. A section icon, if needed = **one horizon line**. Not in the header.

Premium here: **one name, one silhouette, three words**. Not an atlas in a circle.

## 7. How to work (do not skip)

After every picture: a **yes/no table**, not praise. “No” = redraw, do not explain why it is “actually fine”.

**Round A — contour only.**  
Vectorize attachments 2 and 3. Overlay on attachment 1’s lake (the blue shape on the right, not the mountain circle). You may close extraction holes. You may not change proportions or gesture.

1. Is this still the tumentur.ru lake?  
2. At 40 mm, is the edge smooth (not PNG dust and not Lego facets)?  
3. At 24 px, is it not a turd / blob / banana?  
4. Would a 1.5 mm embroidery hold the **body**? If the NE hook fails, say so — do not cut the gesture to pass a checkbox.  
5. Not BBT and not two peaks?

Deliver first: **black contour, 40 mm on white** — the same lake as the site.

**Round B — name.**  
Set «Тумэн Тур». Check э, spacing, «Тур» not a small sticker. Reads as an airport plaque.

**Round C — lockups.**  
Header without the destination line. Letterhead with it.

1. At ~400 px header width, does the name read?  
2. On letterhead, is the line not a slogan blanket and not “three resorts stuck on”?  
3. Without the line, do mark + name still live (mobile)?  
4. No orange in the mark?  
5. Not dirty on `#F7F4EE`?

**Round D — small and avatar.**  
16 / 24 / 32 from a slightly thicker **same** gesture. Telegram 48 / stamp = lake on `#124B56`, no ring.

1. 24 px not a blot?  
2. Avatar is not a compass badge and not BBT?  
3. Alone at 16 px it is honestly a checkmark — write that, do not claim it “is still clearly the lake”.

**Round E — pictograms after the logo, not instead.**  
If Altai becomes a roof or an M — redraw. If three planes still read as “just lines”, keep them and write: destinations are held by **words**.

**Round F — leftovers.**  
Three short sentences: why the outline is still shared among Baikal brands and how the lockup is not BBT / not Krosstur; why the name is empty without the lake and why there is no horde; why the offer is not in the mark.
